Website Changes

Everything changed on the website from the November 2026 review, in plain English. All 41 review comments have been worked through and the changes are live.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

The review left 41 comments on the website. Every one has been worked through and the changes are live. Numbers in brackets match the comment numbers in the review.

1. Homepage

  • New Lawn box: now shows a real natural lawn instead of synthetic turf. (1)
  • Natural Turf card: the dark, fake-looking turf rolls are replaced with a photo of our crew laying turf. (2)
  • Synthetic Turf card: now uses the curved synthetic lawn photo that was picked from the photo folder. (3)
  • Custom Landscaping card: now uses the lit stepping-stone path photo that was picked. (4)
  • "Built Properly" line: the word "drainage" is gone. It now reads "Careful preparation, quality soil and lasting materials." (7)
  • Custom Landscaping box: now explains how we work with the customer, from picturing the space and planning it through to building it and handing it over. (11)
  • Garden Improvements box: now lists the real jobs (patchy lawns, small walls and veggie patches, cleaning synthetic turf, re-mulching, weed spraying, broken edging) and opens a new page for them. (12)
  • New Lawn box link: now goes to the Natural Turf page.
  • Repeated photo: the same mulch photo was on two cards; the Ongoing Property Care card now has its own photo.

2. Every service page

  • Spring Sale box removed from the side of every service page. The Spring Sale stays on the quote form only. (5)
  • "Related services" list removed from the bottom of every service page; it made the pages too busy. (9)
  • Spacing fixed: headings that sat too close to the photos or boxes above them now have the same gap everywhere. This was one fault causing the misalignment on several pages. (10, 15, 34)
  • Placeholder graphics removed: the plain green boxes that looked unfinished are gone from every page. (32, 35)
  • No repeated photos: a photo shown in a before/after slider no longer appears again in the gallery underneath.

3. Turf pages

Split into natural and synthetic

The old "Turf Installation" page mixed real and fake grass on one page. It has been removed, so there is now one page for Natural Turf and one for Synthetic Turf. Anyone using the old link is sent to the Natural Turf page. (6, 14, 17)

Natural Turf page

  • New paragraph, "Why choose natural turf": cooler underfoot, rain soaks in, traps dust, repairs its own wear, safe for kids and pets, cheaper to install than synthetic, and adds value. (18)
  • Buffalo is now described as tough and hardy, not "soft-leaf". (19)
  • Tall fescue is replaced with Seeding. (20)
  • All mention of drainage is removed; the section is now "Ground preparation". (21)

Synthetic Turf page

  • No longer recommended for pet runs. The dog question now says we usually recommend natural turf for dog runs because synthetic can smell. (22)
  • "With correct falls" removed; it now just says "Compacted crushed-rock base". (23)
  • A photo that was actually real grass is removed from the gallery. (24)

4. Other service pages

  • Complete Transformations: the confusing list is replaced with a short written story of the magnolia job: clearing the site, sleeper walls on galvanised posts set in concrete, sandy loam with turf and garden beds around the magnolia, then edging, plants and a letterbox, all done in one week. (8)
  • Paving: the options are cut to two, Concrete pavers and Natural stone (26), and a before/after slider of the stepping-stone entrance path is added (27).
  • Retaining Walls: duplicate photo removed. (28)
  • Garden Design: before/after slider added, using the front garden we designed and built. (30)
  • Landscaping Features: before/after slider added, using the stained sleeper garden. (31)
  • Plants, Garden Beds & Mulch: placeholder graphic replaced with real garden bed photos. (32)
  • Property Maintenance: new photos of real maintenance work (pressure cleaning a patio, a tidy lawn and pebble beds) instead of project photos. (33)
  • Irrigation: placeholder replaced with a real sprinkler photo at the top and in the gallery. (35, 36)
  • Weed Control: the before/after slider now shows a weed-choked garden bed being cleared. (37)
  • New page, Garden Improvements: patchy lawns, small walls and veggie patches, synthetic turf cleaning, re-mulching, weed spraying and replacing broken edging. (12)

5. Pages removed or merged

Old links to these pages still work; each one forwards to the page shown.

  • Turf Installation (mixed natural and synthetic): removed, forwards to Natural Turf. (6)
  • Turf Repair & Patching: removed, forwards to Garden Improvements. (25)
  • Garden Planting: merged into Plants, Garden Beds & Mulch. (13, 16)
  • Timber & Decking: removed from the menu and deleted, forwards to Retaining Walls. (29)

6. Project pages

  • Written descriptions: every project now describes "What we did" as a short written paragraph, step by step, instead of a list of dot points.
  • No repeated photos: galleries no longer repeat the before/after photos shown at the top. (39)
  • Garden lighting project: the second before/after now uses a zoomed-out photo of the finished entrance path. (38)
  • Stained sleeper project: the near-duplicate photo is replaced with a front-on view of the finished wall. (40)
  • Turf projects: the two turf projects are different jobs at different houses, so the first is renamed "Front Yard Natural Turf Installation" to tell it apart from the backyard one. (41)
  • Magnolia project: now says it was finished in one week.
  • Service links: the "Services on this project" boxes now point to Natural Turf or Synthetic Turf instead of the old mixed turf page.

7. New photos

Seven photos came from the Bastiano Media photo folder:

  • Curved synthetic lawn beside a brick path: homepage Synthetic Turf card.
  • Lit stepping-stone path at dusk: homepage Custom Landscaping card.
  • Wider dusk shot of the same entrance path: Paving slider and the garden lighting project.
  • Front-on view of the stained sleeper wall: stained sleeper project.
  • Pressure cleaning a patio: Property Maintenance.
  • Tidy lawn with white pebble beds: Property Maintenance and the homepage Ongoing Property Care card.

Other photo changes reuse real photos that were already on the website, moved to where they fit better.

8. How it was checked

  • Every page on the live website was loaded and checked: all pages work, and no links or images are broken.
  • Every old link to a removed page was tested and forwards to the right place.
  • Each of the 41 review comments was checked against the live website.
  • Pages were checked on both desktop and phone screen sizes.
