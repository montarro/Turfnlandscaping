# How much does natural turf installation cost in Melbourne?

The honest answer is that a new natural lawn is priced on the ground it goes onto, not just on the turf. Two lawns of the same size can be quoted very differently because one is bare, level soil and the other is an old lawn on compacted clay with no way to get a machine in. This guide explains what sits inside an installed price, what moves it up or down, and what to have ready so a quote can be accurate.

For a straightforward lawn on prepared ground the turf itself is a smaller share of the bill than most people expect. Removal, soil, levelling and the labour to lay and roll the turf make up the rest. Once the examples are in place, this section will show how the totals on real jobs related to their area, and why the price per square metre drops as the lawn gets larger.

## Supply only, or supplied and installed

Turf growers sell by the square metre, and delivery is usually charged on top. A supply only price covers the rolls or slabs dropped at your kerb and nothing else: no removal of what is there, no soil, no laying, and no responsibility for how the lawn establishes.

A supplied and installed price covers the whole job: the site cleared, the soil prepared and levelled, the turf laid, rolled and watered in, and establishment instructions handed over. When you compare quotes, make sure both are for the same thing.

## What a proper installed price includes

Our [natural turf installation](/services/natural-turf-installation) quotes cover the stages that decide whether a lawn is still good in two years:

- **Clearing.** Old lawn, weeds and rubbish removed, with the old surface treated so it does not grow back through the new turf.
- **Disposal.** Spoil and old turf carted away. Tip fees are a real cost on larger jobs.
- **Soil.** Topsoil imported and spread where the existing soil is thin, and gypsum or sandy loam worked in where Melbourne's clay needs it.
- **Levelling and falls.** The base graded so water sheds away from the house and the lawn can be mown evenly.
- **Drainage.** Soggy spots dealt with before the turf goes down, not after it dies.
- **Turf, laying and rolling.** Fresh turf laid with staggered joints and rolled for soil contact.
- **Watering in and handover.** The first watering done, with simple care instructions for your variety and the season.

If a quote is noticeably cheaper, check which of these has been left out. [Turf preparation, levelling and drainage](/services/turf-preparation-levelling-drainage) is the part most often skipped, and it is the part that decides whether [new turf fails](/blog/why-new-turf-fails).

## What changes the price

**Area and shape.** Turf is bought by the square metre, so area is the starting point. Curved edges, narrow strips and lots of obstacles mean more cutting and more waste than a plain rectangle.

**What is there now.** Bare, level soil is the cheapest starting point. An existing lawn has to be removed and treated. Paving, concrete or builders' rubble has to be broken up and carted away.

**Soil condition.** Much of Melbourne's west and north sits on heavy clay. If the soil is thin, compacted or waterlogged, imported topsoil and soil conditioners are part of the job.

**Access.** If a small machine can get into the yard, soil and spoil move quickly. If everything has to go through the house or a narrow side gate by wheelbarrow, the labour goes up.

**Levels and drainage.** A yard that needs regrading, or that holds water after rain, needs more work below the surface before any turf arrives.

**Variety.** Growers price varieties differently, and availability changes through the year. Soft-leaf buffalo, couch, kikuyu and tall fescue each suit different sites, so the right choice is about sun and use first and price second.

**Timing.** Turf is a living product that is cut to order. The season affects availability and how much watering the new lawn needs to establish, which is covered in our guide to [the best time to lay turf in Melbourne](/blog/best-time-to-lay-turf-melbourne).

## Why small lawns cost more per square metre

A small front lawn still needs a site visit, a turf delivery, a soil delivery, the same tools and the same tip run as a big one. Those fixed costs are spread over fewer square metres, so the rate per metre is higher even though the total is lower. That is normal, and it is why a quote for a courtyard lawn will not match a per metre figure quoted for a large backyard.

## What a turf quote usually excludes

Unless they are listed, these are separate jobs: irrigation installation or sprinkler repairs, retaining walls or new edging, fence or paving repairs, removing trees or stumps, and ongoing mowing. Ask for them to be quoted at the same time if you want them done together.
