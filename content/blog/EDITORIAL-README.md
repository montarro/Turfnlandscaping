# Bastiano Landscaping SEO Blog Library

Prepared for `bastianolandscaping.com.au` in Australian English.

## Recommended scale

Publish the 12 launch articles over the first 12–16 weeks, then publish two strong articles per month. Do not release dozens of thin posts at once. Each article should answer a real customer question, support a relevant service page, use genuine project imagery where possible and end with a helpful next step.

The 12 launch articles are complete drafts. The editorial roadmap contains the next 24 topics, giving the site a full 12-month pipeline without creating unnecessary content.

## Before publishing

- Confirm the suggested internal-link paths against the live website routes.
- Have Sebastian review technical advice and add a short real-project observation where relevant.
- Use the assigned real image, not unrelated stock imagery.
- Compress each image and supply the provided descriptive alt text.
- Add a visible author line: **By Sebastian Caus, owner of Bastiano Landscaping**.
- Include `Article` or `BlogPosting` structured data, breadcrumbs and a visible last-reviewed date.
- Keep the service page as the main commercial page. The blog should educate and link to it, not repeat it word for word.
- Update an article when advice, services or examples change. Do not change dates merely to make old content look fresh.

## Internal-link placeholders

The drafts use likely paths such as `/services/natural-turf` and `/quote`. If the live routes differ, update them before publishing. Every article should link to one core service page, one related article and the quote page where contextually appropriate.

## Local SEO guardrail

Do not mass-produce articles that only swap suburb names. A suburb article is worth publishing only when it contains genuinely local information, a completed project or photograph from that area, property/context observations, and a useful answer not already covered elsewhere.

## Tone

Helpful, practical, premium and straightforward. Avoid exaggerated claims, invented project details, unverified prices, fake urgency and phrases such as “best in Melbourne”. The owner-led advantage should come through naturally: customers can speak directly with Sebastian about what suits their property.
